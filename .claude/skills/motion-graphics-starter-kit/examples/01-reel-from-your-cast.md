# 1. A reel from your own products and characters

You have a folder of products, mascots or characters (logos, packshots, character art, finished ads) and want one
punchy reel that shows them all. Replace the bracket with your folder.

```
Build me a motion graphics video in the claymation style. Use all the products and characters from [folder, e.g. assets/brand/]. Add music and sound effects so it really pops. Render it to MP4.
```

What to expect: every image in the folder gets looked at, each product or character gets its own beat, and the
film ends on the brand. No length given, so it comes out at about 15 seconds. Swap *claymation* for any look you
like: *flat graphic*, *premium dark*, *paper cut-out*.
