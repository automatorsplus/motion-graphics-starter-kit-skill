# 3. A launch film for an app, in the style of videos you like

The hardest of the three: the film has to learn a style from examples. Replace the brackets with your app, one
line on what it does, two or three links to motion graphics you love, and your tagline.

```
Make a 15-second launch film for [app, and one line on what it does]. It needs outstanding motion graphics that really stand out, not something basic or corporate. Look at the motion graphics in these example videos: [links], and build it in that style with HyperFrames. End on the name and the line: [tagline]. Add music and sound effects cut to the beats. Render it to MP4.
```

What to expect: Claude pulls frames from each example, writes down what makes the style (palette, type, how
things move, how fast it cuts) and builds to those notes. Watching links needs `yt-dlp`; the skill offers to
install it if it is missing. If the first cut is close but not there, say what is off in one line and ask for
another pass.
