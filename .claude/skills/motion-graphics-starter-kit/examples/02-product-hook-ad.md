# 2. One product, a hook ad with a wow factor

One product, about 15 seconds, built to stop the scroll or hold the first seconds of a video. Replace the bracket
with your product, and attach a product photo if you have one.

```
Make a really insane, highly creative ad / motion graphics video for [product, e.g. my hot sauce, attached] in the claymation style, about 15 seconds. It needs a real wow factor: this is the hook at the start of a YouTube video, so it has to keep viewers hooked. Add music and sound effects so it really pops.
```

What to expect: the most striking image in the first second, one idea per beat, a held breath before the biggest
move, and the product landing on the end card. If you attach a photo, it is used as given, never redrawn. With Higgsfield set up and a yes from you, the scenes can be generated claymation sets around your product, like the hot sauce ads in the video; without it they are built in code.
